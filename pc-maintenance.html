PC Maintenance

Computer maintenance involves keeping a computer system in good working condition by cleaning, inspecting, updating and troubleshooting its hardware and software.

1. What is PC Maintenance?

PC maintenance is the process of taking care of computer hardware and software to ensure that the computer works properly, efficiently and reliably.

2. Types of PC Maintenance

Preventive Maintenance

Preventive maintenance is performed regularly to prevent computer problems before they occur.

  • Cleaning dust from the computer.
  • Checking cables and connections.
  • Updating Windows and software.
  • Checking storage space.
  • Scanning for viruses and malware.

Corrective Maintenance

Corrective maintenance involves identifying and fixing problems that have already occurred.

  • Replacing faulty RAM.
  • Replacing a damaged hard disk or SSD.
  • Fixing overheating problems.
  • Repairing software problems.
  • Fixing boot problems.

3. Common PC Problems

  • Computer does not turn on.
  • Computer is running slowly.
  • No display on the monitor.
  • Computer overheating.
  • Windows fails to boot.
  • Keyboard or mouse does not work.
  • Computer shuts down unexpectedly.

4. Basic Troubleshooting Procedure

  1. Identify the problem.
  2. Check power connections.
  3. Check cables and hardware components.
  4. Restart the computer.
  5. Check Windows and drivers.
  6. Test the faulty component.
  7. Repair or replace the faulty component.
  8. Test the computer again.

5. Tools Used in PC Maintenance

  • Screwdriver set
  • Multimeter
  • Compressed air
  • Cleaning brush
  • Thermal paste
  • USB flash drive

6. Importance of PC Maintenance

  • Improves computer performance.
  • Prevents hardware failure.
  • Reduces downtime.
  • Extends computer lifespan.
  • Improves system reliability.
